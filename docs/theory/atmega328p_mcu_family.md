# ATmega328 MCU Family

ATmega328 is a microcontroller family provided by Microchip. These types of microcontrollers are 8-bit and part of the AVR brand.

The ATmega328P became widely known in part because it was used as the main microcontroller on popular Arduino boards such as the Arduino Uno Rev3.
