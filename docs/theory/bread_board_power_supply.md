# Breadboard Power Supply

A breadboard power supply module provides power to a breadboard from an external source, such as a USB supply or wall adapter. Many modules provide regulated 5 V and 3.3 V outputs, which are common supply voltages for digital circuits. Check that the voltage matches the requirements of the components in your circuit.

These modules are convenient and usually less expensive than a laboratory power supply. Their available power depends on the external source and the module’s limits.
